How it works

Three steps.
Zero friction.

01
Check before you go

Open EcoTrail and search your destination. See real-time AQI, crowd levels, and community hazard reports all in one view.

02
Report what you see

Spot pollution on the trail? Upload a photo. AI classifies it and the hazard appears on the live map within seconds.

03
Ask the chatbot

Questions about eco-guidelines, safe alternatives, or local conditions? EcoBot gives instant answers from a verified knowledge base.
